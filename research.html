What I’ve built

Beyond the studies above, I built the software that runs them.

Founder & developer
Covexe covexe.com

One platform for the whole systematic review, from protocol to publication figures: planning, searching, screening, risk-of-bias appraisal, AI-assisted data extraction, meta-analysis and a full statistics suite, all in one browser. AI stays assistive: every extracted value cites the exact sentence it came from, and the statistics run on your own machine, so your data never leaves it.

40+methods checked against R
17+tools, search to synthesis

Meta-analysis & NMA PRISMA / CONSORT RoB2 · ROBINS-I · GRADE AI extraction, cited to source Client-side statistics
Free core, validated maths. The statistics, appraisal and reporting tools are free; a methods paper validating the engine against R (metafor, survival, MASS) is in preparation with faculty co-authors.
