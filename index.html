01 About關於

Who I am, what I do 我是誰，做什麼

I am a doctoral student at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU) in Taipei. My research covers global cancer epidemiology, obesity and non-communicable disease trends, and burden of disease. I have worked as a data analyst with hospitals and cancer centers in Taiwan. 我是國立陽明交通大學（NYCU）博士生，駐台北。研究主題包含全球癌症流行病學、肥胖與非傳染性疾病趨勢，以及疾病負擔。我曾在台灣多家醫院及癌症中心擔任資料分析師。

I work with data from GBD, GLOBOCAN, the NCD Risk Factor Collaboration, and WHO. One dataset I processed had over 18 million rows. I run analysis in R, Stata, and Joinpoint, support manuscript drafting, and use SPSS when a client needs it. 我處理 GBD、GLOBOCAN、NCD Risk Factor Collaboration 及 WHO 的資料，曾處理超過 1,800 萬筆的單一資料集。分析使用 R、Stata 及 Joinpoint，提供論文撰寫支援，也可依客戶需求使用 SPSS。

02 Services服務項目

What I offer 服務內容

01
Statistical analysis統計分析
Regression (linear, logistic, Poisson), joinpoint regression, time series, survival analysis, mixed models. R, Stata, or Joinpoint depending on the method. 迴歸分析（線性、邏輯斯、卜瓦松）、接合點迴歸、時間序列、存活分析、混合模型。依方法使用 R、Stata 或 Joinpoint。
02
Figures in R圖表製作（R）
Choropleth maps, heatmaps, spaghetti plots, dumbbell charts, lollipop plots, scatter composites, stacked bars, faceted panels. Built in ggplot2, sf, and tmap to journal specifications. 等值地圖、熱圖、折線圖、啞鈴圖、棒棒糖圖、散點複合圖、堆疊長條圖、分面圖。以 ggplot2、sf 及 tmap 依期刊規格製作。
03
Epidemiological analysis流行病學分析
Burden of disease, AAPC computation, age-period-cohort analysis, SDI stratification, sex-disaggregated comparison, cross-country trends using GBD, GLOBOCAN, and WHO data. 疾病負擔、AAPC 計算、年齡-時期-世代分析、SDI 分層、性別分層比較、使用 GBD、GLOBOCAN 及 WHO 資料的跨國趨勢分析。
04
Large dataset processing大型資料集處理
Cleaning, restructuring, and analysis at scale. Familiar with GBD, GLOBOCAN, NCD-RisC, UN WPP, and WHO indicator formats. Handled a single dataset over 18 million rows. 大規模資料清理、重組與分析。熟悉 GBD、GLOBOCAN、NCD-RisC、聯合國 WPP 及 WHO 指標格式，曾處理超過 1,800 萬筆的單一資料集。
05
Manuscript support論文撰寫支援
Methods sections, results drafting, statistical reporting, table formatting, and figure preparation for submission. I also help with revision responses. 方法學撰寫、結果陳述、統計報告、表格排版及圖形準備，協助投稿。也可協助回應審查意見。
06
Reports and slides報告與簡報
Conference decks, clinical summaries, policy briefs, and reports with embedded figures. R Markdown, PowerPoint, or HTML. 學術會議簡報、臨床摘要、政策報告及嵌入圖表的研究報告。可用 R Markdown、PowerPoint 或 HTML 產出。
